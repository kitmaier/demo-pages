Cube Distances Coloring Puzzle

This is based on a puzzle introduced in a 3Blue1Brown video.

The puzzle is for a 4x4x4 grid of 64 small cubes which are colored with 3 colors, find 6 nonoverlapping pairs of small cubes which are all the same color and where the distances between the centers of the cubes in the pairs are all the same.

First, by the pigeonhole principle, as there are 3 colors and 64 = 3*21+1 cubes, it must be the case that there are at least 22 cubes of some color. We can consider only the first 22 cubes of that color going forward.

Of those cubes, there are (22 choose 2) or 22*21/2 = 231 pairs of cubes, each with some distance between the cubes.

In the overall 4x4x4 grid, there are only 18 distinct distances between small cubes, the total table of counts of distances is below.

By the pigeonhole principle again, since 231 = 18*12+15, there must be at least one distance which shows up in at least 13 pairs of the 22 cubes, although we only really need 12.

Of these 12 pairs, there must be at least 6 pairs that are nonoverlapping. Any particular small cube can only show up in at most two of these pairs.
